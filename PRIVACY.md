# Anky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

## The short version

Anky keeps what you log on your iPhone. It has no accounts, servers or analytics and does not send your log anywhere,
so the developer has no access to it. Anky is made by Tëma Bolshakov, an individual developer.

## What Anky stores

Whatever you enter in Anky, such as symptoms, medications and doses, notes, and questionnaire results, along with their
dates and times. This is health information. It is stored only on your device. Reports and exports are made on your
phone from this same information.

## What is collected

Anky does not collect or transmit what you log, and the developer has no access to it. There is no account, no
advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

The exception is anything you send to the developer yourself, such as an email or a screenshot sent through TestFlight
feedback. Please do not include health details. If you do, the developer uses them only to reply and deletes them
afterwards.

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may give the developer aggregated crash
statistics. They are not used to identify anyone.

## How your data is used and kept

Anky uses your entries only to show them back to you. No third party receives your data. It stays on your device until
you delete it.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. They are optional, and nothing is sent through a server. Reminder text can
include a medication name and appears on your lock screen and on a paired Apple Watch; you can hide previews in iOS
Settings > Notifications. To stop reminders, turn off notifications for Anky in iOS Settings.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you send it. Exporting your data or a report opens the iOS share sheet, and the file goes where you choose.
After that, the place you chose (Files, iCloud Drive, email, your doctor's system) is responsible for it, not Anky.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Anky's data may be included in that backup. Apple and your own
settings control that.

## Your control

You can edit or delete any entry in History. "Erase all data" in Settings deletes everything stored inside Anky,
including scheduled reminders. Deleting the app does the same. Copies you exported, and any device backup made earlier,
are not touched; delete those where they are stored.

The developer holds no copy, so cannot access, correct or recover your data. You manage it in the app, so keep a
backup if you want one. You can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or to your local
authority.

## Not medical advice

Anky is a personal log. It does not give medical advice. Check with your doctor before making medical decisions.

## Children

Anky is not aimed at children.

## Open source

Anky's source code is public, and the source of each release is tagged at [repository URL], so you can check how it
handles data.

## Changes

This policy covers Anky. If a version changes how data is handled, the developer will update this page and its date no
later than the release of that version, and describe what changed here. Features that send data off your device will be
off until you turn them on.
